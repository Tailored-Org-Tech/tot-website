02 / HOW I WORK

The problem comes first

Listen closely.
Build what makes sense.

I start with your business, not a preferred platform. Sometimes the right answer is a new tool. Sometimes it’s a better use of what you already have.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Understand the people, the process, and what’s getting in the way.

  2. 02

    Measure

    Look at how often the problem happens and what it costs in time and effort.

  3. 03

    Design

    Find a straightforward solution that fits the way your team works.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Build, implement, and support the tool as it becomes part of your day.

03 / ABOUT

A practical perspective

Technology should
make work simpler.

I run TOT Software, a Missouri-based software development and technology consulting business focused on the everyday needs of small businesses.

I believe useful technology starts with understanding the operation. Before recommending a platform or writing code, I want to learn where time is lost, which tasks are frustrating, and what a better process would look like.

The goal is useful software without unnecessary complexity. Something that fits your business, rather than asking your business to fit the software.

Business problem first. Technology second.
